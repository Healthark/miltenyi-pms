Project Goals for Miltenyi

Based on the 2 Sep 2026 meeting · gap review

The idea in one line

One table per employee for the year. Rows are the KPIs of their framework; the columns are KPI · Goal · Self review · PM review. The framework paragraphs sit above the table. The same table is what the employee, the Healthark reviewer and HR see; only the column that is editable changes with the stage, and each column fills in when its step happens. Ratings sit in a footer row.

  • •The Prototype controls panel (bottom right) switches the persona, the stage of the goal set, weightage visibility, rating visibility and who gives the final rating. Every screen reacts.
  • •Elements marked to confirm are positions the prototype takes on an open question; strike or keep them during the review.
  • •Chrome, colours, type and components are the React app's own. Nothing saves data; buttons that advance the stage just move the prototype forward.

Framework mapping and content

Admin Panel. Framework Mapping: employee, function, GCC designation and level, reviewer (mentor), Miltenyi reviewer, status. Framework: per function, a matrix of Level 1–4 columns with the two illustrative paragraphs and the KPI + weightage list per level, staged Save/Discard, and "Add Function" to add a role/level row with its KPI table.

Positions this prototype takes

  • to confirmOne goal set per employee for CY 2026; the per-project PM review queue retires for Miltenyi, so "Project Reviews" is absent from the menu.
  • to confirmSubmit records an offline agreement; the reviewer's "Mark approved" click locks the Goal column. No comment loop.
  • to confirmEvery KPI row needs a goal to submit.
  • to confirmThe PM review column holds the Miltenyi reviewer's words; a small optional Healthark note can sit under them. One self rating and one final rating in the footer; whose final rating it is, is a toggle.
  • to confirmCY 2026 has a single evaluation after 31 Dec; the stage strip shows one review, not quarters.
  • to confirmWeightages are read-only and their visibility to employees is a switch; ratings visibility to employees is a switch.
  • to confirmThe employee acknowledges the review with one click.

Not in these wireframes

Notifications, dashboards, exports and the mentor's view of project goals. The reviewer packet (the file handed to the Miltenyi reviewer) is a preview only.
